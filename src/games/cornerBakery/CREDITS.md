# Corner Bakery Rush: credits

## 3D models
**Kenney** (www.kenney.nl), licensed **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain):
- Food Kit 2.0: https://kenney.nl/assets/food-kit
- Furniture Kit 2.0: https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit

The License.txt in each kit reads "License: (Creative Commons Zero, CC0)".

`assets/bakery-props.glb` repacks 44 of these models: 22 food and 22 furniture.
- The Food Kit colormap is embedded, and the meshes are meshopt-compressed (EXT_meshopt_compression, KHR_mesh_quantization).
- Each model is a top-level node named after its source file.
- The repack was made with @gltf-transform (MIT) in a scratch folder outside the repo.

CC0 needs no attribution. It is given here as thanks.

## Everything else is original to this game
- The bakery building, street, customers, textures and menu-card art: procedural Three.js geometry and canvas drawings.
- Music ("Morning Rush") and all sound effects: synthesized live with Web Audio. No samples or audio files are used.
