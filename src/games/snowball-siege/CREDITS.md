# Snowball Siege — credits
- Design, code, arena, procedural audio fallback: Iceberg Game Maker 2 (QoL update, 2026-10-31).
- Audio cues (to be replaced by Sound Designer pack in `./sfx/<cue>.ogg`): bgm_snowball-siege, bgm_siege-lobby,
  sfx_throw_charge, sfx_throw_release, sfx_snowball_hit, sfx_snowball_whiff, sfx_fort_hit, sfx_fort_break,
  sfx_capture_tick, sfx_capture_win, sfx_respawn, sfx_reload, sfx_cheer, sfx_defeat.
  Drop files with those names into `sfx/` — they load automatically; procedural synth plays until then.
- Patterns borrowed from Knockout Floes (pooled snowballs, particles, text pops) and Rimebound mobile UX.
- Three.js (MIT).
