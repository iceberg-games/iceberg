ICEBERG - how to play
=====================

1. Open the "iceberg" folder and double-click  index.html
   (Chrome or Edge works best.)
2. Click START, then pick a game and press PLAY.

Controls
  W A S D  = walk          Space = jump
  Drag the mouse = look around    Scroll = zoom
  B = build mode (in Frost Fields): click to place a block,
      Shift+click or right-click to remove one. Keys 1-9 pick a block.
  T = Shop   L = Locker   M = Models   H = Help
  F = fly (only when you wear the Ice Rocket)

Birg Bucks (BB) are pretend money. You get them by:
  - grabbing coins (+5)
  - building (+1 per block, up to 50 a day)
  - the daily bonus (+100)
Spend them in the Shop on hats, skins, wings, the Ice Rocket and more!

Make your own game
  On the Games screen, press CREATE. Place blocks, coins and a spawn
  point, choose the sky and music, then press SAVE & PUBLISH. Your game shows up
  under "Your Games". Use EXPORT to save it as a file you can share,
  and IMPORT to load one.

Your stuff is saved in this browser on this computer. If you use a
different browser, you start fresh.
