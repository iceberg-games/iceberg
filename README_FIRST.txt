ICEBERG - how to play
=====================

1. Open the "iceberg" folder and double-click  index.html
   (Chrome or Edge works best.)
2. Click START, then pick a game and press PLAY.

Controls
  W A S D  = walk          Space = jump
  Drag the mouse = look around    Scroll = zoom
  B = build mode (in Frost Fields): click to place a block,
      Shift+click or right-click to remove one. Keys 1-9 pick a block.
  T = Shop   L = Locker   M = Models   H = Help
  F = fly (only when you wear the Ice Rocket)

Birg Bucks (BB) are pretend money. You get them by:
  - grabbing coins (+5)
  - building (+1 per block, up to 50 a day)
  - the daily bonus (+100)
Spend them in the Shop on hats, skins, wings, the Ice Rocket and more!

Make your own game (Studio)
  On the Games screen, press CREATE (computer only).
  - Right-click + drag to look around, WASD to fly.
  - Insert blocks, balls, cylinders, wedges, a spawn point,
    checkpoints, lava, coins and gears from the top bar or the Toolbox.
  - Click a part to select it. Use Move / Scale / Rotate (keys 2 3 4)
    and change its color and material on the right.
  - Shift-click or drag a box to select several parts. Ctrl+A selects
    everything. Ctrl+G groups them into a Model, Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V copy
    and paste.
  - "+ Add script" lets you type what a part should do in plain words,
    like "spin slowly" or "when touched give 10 points". The PHRASES
    tab lists ideas you can click.
  - Ctrl+Z undoes a mistake. Press TEST to try your game.
  - SAVE keeps your work. PUBLISH puts it under "Your Games".
  - EXPORT saves it as a file you can share, and IMPORT loads one.

Your stuff is saved in this browser on this computer. If you use a
different browser, you start fresh.
