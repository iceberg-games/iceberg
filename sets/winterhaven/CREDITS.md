# Winterhaven: credits
Everything in this set is Iceberg original:
- Procedural Three.js geometry: cabins, tree, castle, pines, stalls, cave, snowmen
- GLSL patterns: logs, stone, cobbles, ice blocks, sky with stars, aurora and moon, snow, smoke
- Canvas textures: skate marks and frost on the lake

No external models, textures or audio are used, and nothing comes from Roblox. If CC0 assets are added later (for example the Kenney packs credited in the Corner Bakery game), copy them into `sets/winterhaven/assets/` and list them here.
